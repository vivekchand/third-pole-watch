## Rules of engagement

- Lead with the running instrument, not the vision: replay plot, live site,
  audit repo. Ask for advice before asking for anything else.
- Never present the watch as an official warning service — to anyone.
- Log every contact and outcome here (date, channel, response) — the
  outreach ledger deserves the same honesty as the candidate ledger.
